Core Services

A Studio Built for Results

VEXUS was founded on 4 February 2025. We started as a 3D-focused studio, expanded into video and photography, and later added development. To date we've completed around 25 projects valued at over $7,000, including a book and an educational website in Saudi Arabia, architectural work in Saudi Arabia and Morocco, video productions in Palestine, and voice-over work in Jordan. We deliver books, educational content, and complete visual identities, and we currently produce thumbnails and media content for recurring clients. Founders & CEOs: Abdelrahman Yahya & Abdelrahman Ahmed lead projects from concept to delivery.
